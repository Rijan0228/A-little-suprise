Dear Dipika,

I have been trying to find the right words, but maybe there aren't perfect words for something that feels this genuine.

When we first started talking on Instagram, I had no idea that a random conversation would turn into someone so important to me. I didn't plan it. It just happened, little by little. A simple hello became a reason to check my phone and a conversation became something I looked forward to.

You were there for me when I felt like nobody was. You listened, you understood me, and you made me feel that I didn't have to explain every little thing to be understood. I hope I have made you feel even a little of that comfort too.

I appreciate the person you are, not just the way you make me feel. Your kindness, your honesty, your way of understanding things, and all those little moments that might seem ordinary to you are meaningful to me.

I like how we can understand each other in ways that don't always need a long explanation. I value the conversations, the laughter, the small check-ins, and even the quiet moments. They may look ordinary from the outside, but they have their own place in my heart.

There is something I want to say clearly: I love you. I don't say it to put pressure on you or to ask you to say it back. I say it because it's what I genuinely feel, and I wanted you to know.

I don't expect you to have an answer ready. Take your time, be honest with yourself, and tell me what you really feel whenever you're comfortable. You don't have to match my feelings just because I've shared them. Your comfort and honesty matter more than any particular answer.

Whatever you feel, I will respect it. I never want these words to become a weight on your shoulders. I only hope they help you understand how much I appreciate having you in my life.

Thank you for being someone who made a difference in my life. I'm really glad our paths crossed, and I hope life gives you countless reasons to smile.

With care,

Someone who thinks you're special ♡
